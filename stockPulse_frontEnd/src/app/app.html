Rule-Based Engine (Deterministic)

BUILT-IN

Fast, predictable, and deterministic rule algorithms that analyze inventory ratios and demand statistics:

  • Pricing Rule: If stock is at or below reorder threshold, recommends a +10% price increase to regulate depletion.
  • Demand Spike Rule: If demand velocity exceeds 3x category average, recommends a +5% price increase.
  • Replenishment Formula: Recommends reorder quantity = max(1, reorderThreshold × 3 - currentStock).
  • Zero external API dependencies; runs locally on the Spring Boot server.

AI Advisor (LLM Powered)

INTELLIGENT

Powered by a LiteLLM/OpenAI-compatible language model configured in Spring Boot:

  • Analyzes product category context, demand elasticity, and market conditions.
  • Synthesizes natural language reasoning explaining economic trade-offs.
  • Outputs confidence scores and recommended lead time projections.
  • Gracefully falls back to Rule-Based Advisor if LLM credentials are unavailable.
